Innovations in kinetic therapy and lower-limb biomechanics.

Flagship product

Multi-Ax-Sys

The Multi-Ax-Sys is a semi-open, multi-axis leg–ankle–foot system designed to support, guide, and resist natural foot motion or gestures—minimizing inappropriate external forces while enabling multi-planar motion. Built on decades of biomechanical research and a foundational U.S. patent, it targets strengthening, rehabilitation, and clinical assessment of the lower limb kinetic chain.

Key features & benefits

  • Enhanced Lower Limb Function

    Support and assistance during movement, promoting proper alignment and optimal muscle activation patterns.

  • Injury Prevention

    Reduced stress on the lower limb kinetic chain helps prevent injuries and overuse conditions.

  • Performance Optimization

    Improved agility, balance, and power output for athletes and individuals seeking performance enhancement.

  • Customizability & Adaptability

    Adjustable for rehabilitation, sports training, clinical research, and daily functional applications.

We envision Multi-Ax-Sys as a cornerstone of lower-limb rehabilitation and kinetic therapy—giving health professionals technology that respects neurophysiological control, multi-axis foot function, and the real demands of gait, sport, and recovery.
